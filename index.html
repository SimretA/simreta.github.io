I study how humans and AI learn together.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Toby Li. I design and evaluate tools that foster human-AI collaboration in ambiguous or uncertain contexts. Grounded in cognitive learning theories, my work helps people align their intentions, preferences, and values during model training, fine-tuning, and evaluation.

How can humans remain active partners in shaping AI behavior?

My research follows the relationship between humans and AI across three connected activities: teaching models, evaluating their behavior, and maintaining meaningful human oversight.

01

Teach

How can users communicate concepts and preferences to AI?

I build learning-centered interfaces that help people express knowledge and refine it alongside a model.

How can users recognize and correct agent misalignment?

I study how agent behavior reflects human values and how oversight can help users identify and address harmful behavior.
